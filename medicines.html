Medicine Encyclopedia

Comprehensive guide to common medications, their uses, and trusted medical sources.

💊
Analgesics (Pain Relievers)
Analgesics are medicines that relieve pain without loss of consciousness. They range from OTC options like Aspirin and Acetaminophen to prescription drugs.

Common Uses: Headaches, muscle aches, arthritis, dental pain.
MedlinePlus →
🦠
Antibiotics
Powerful medicines that fight bacterial infections and save lives when used properly.

Important: Antibiotics do NOT work on viral infections like colds and flu.
CDC Guidelines →
🤧
Antihistamines
Drugs that treat allergy symptoms by blocking histamine, a chemical released by the immune system.

Common Uses: Seasonal allergies, hives, insect bites.
Mayo Clinic →
🌡️
Antipyretics
Substances that reduce fever by acting on the hypothalamus to lower body temperature.

Examples: Ibuprofen, Paracetamol.
WebMD Guide →
🧴
Antiseptics
Antimicrobial substances applied to skin to reduce infection risk. Essential for first-aid kits.

Common Uses: Cleaning cuts, scratches, surgical wounds.
Healthline →
🍊
Vitamins & Supplements
Organic compounds needed in small quantities, mostly from food or supplementation.

Key Types: Vitamin C (Immunity), Vitamin D (Bones), B12 (Energy).
